Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about practicing speaking with WeTalksy.

How does WeTalksy help me practice speaking instead of just memorizing? +
WeTalksy incorporates immediate audio playback, phoneme-level acoustic scoring, and real-life scenario dialogs. Rather than matching static text choices, you listen to native speech and speak back in complete sentences.
Can I practice if I am a complete beginner (A1 Level)? +
Yes! WeTalksy starts with essential greetings, phonetic guides, and slow native pronunciation playback, allowing beginners to build confidence gradually.
What languages are supported on WeTalksy? +
We currently support Spanish, English, French, German, Japanese, Korean, and Italian, with more languages being added regularly.
